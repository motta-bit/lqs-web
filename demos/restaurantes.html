A delivery paid on arrival gets cancelled; one already paid does not. The customer pays by card, Nequi or bank debit, and the kitchen starts with the money already in.

  • Tarjeta, Nequi y PSE en la misma pantalla. Card, Nequi and PSE on the same screen.
